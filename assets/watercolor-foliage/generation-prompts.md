# Watercolor foliage assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Both PNGs preserve their alpha channels. These are original generated assets for the procedural meadow; they are not yet wired into the website.

## Leaves atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production botanical watercolor asset atlas on a genuinely transparent background: exactly 12 separate isolated individual leaves, in a spacious 4-column 3-row arrangement, each fully visible and not touching any other leaf. Each leaf naturally different: broad oval, long pointed, gently curled, tilted side-on, young small leaf, slightly asymmetric mature leaf. Restrained pale sage green, muted celadon, faint blue green, soft yellow olive. Hand-painted refined botanical watercolor, delicate pigment granulation INSIDE leaves, transparent uneven washes, subtle central veins and occasional fine secondary veins, gently irregular contours, lovely natural organic shapes. Similar to a light dreamlike watercolor garden border on white. Light but enough visible pigment to composite and reduce opacity later. No background paper, no white rectangles, no checkerboard baked in, no shadows, no text, no labels, no border, no tree, no full scene. Generous empty transparent margins around each isolated leaf. Not vector, not geometric, not thick outlines, not glossy. Each leaf with a tiny narrow petiole suitable for attaching to a procedural twig.

## Twigs atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production watercolor botanical sprite atlas on genuinely transparent background: six separate isolated SMALL LEAFY TWIG illustrations arranged with generous space in 3 columns and 2 rows. These are attachable foliage modules, NOT whole trees or bouquets. Each twig starts at a bare thin cut base at its lower left or lower centre, curves gently upward and splits into 2 or 3 fine asymmetrical offshoots with 7 to 14 organically varied leaves. Six distinctly different silhouettes: airy upward spray, gently arching sideways twig, loose forked spray, short rounded cluster, slender reaching spray, delicate hanging spray. Pale sage green, celadon, misty blue green, subtle yellow olive; thin warm grey olive wood. Refined hand-painted botanical watercolor with delicate pigment granulation inside leaves, translucent overlapping leaves, softly irregular edges, fine veins and naturally tapered curved twigs. Elegant dreamy quiet delicate appearance matching a pale watercolor garden illustration. Keep visible soft pigmentation that can be faded down by software later. Generous transparent gaps between all six illustrations, all tips fully visible. No paper background, no white rectangles, no baked checkerboard, no text, no labels, no shadows, no frames, no trunk, no ground. No vector geometry or repeated identical leaves.
