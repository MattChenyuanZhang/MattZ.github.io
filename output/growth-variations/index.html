Six ways a wish can grow.

Compare the sequence and rhythm. All use faint colors and small plants along the bottom edge.
Previews run at 18× speed. Some sequences are deliberately poetic rather than botanical.

1. Natural unfolding

Full size

Recommended. Seed rests, two leaves emerge, a stalk rises, then flower → puff → release. The closest to a natural life cycle.

2. Dreamlike unfurling

Full size

Leaves open slowly, then the stem curls upward and straightens. A more fluid, storybook feeling.

3. A little bow

Full size

The growing flower leans gently before standing tall. At release, it gives a small bow and returns to stillness.

4. Two gentle blooms

Full size

The plant blooms, rests, then opens once more before becoming a puff. A soft second moment of joy.

5. Quiet rosette

Full size

The leaves stay low for most of the cycle. A short flower appears late, then fades. The least distracting option.

6. Wish and dissolve

Full size

A tiny puff forms directly after the leaves, releases its wishes, then the whole plant dissolves slowly. More poetic than botanical.
